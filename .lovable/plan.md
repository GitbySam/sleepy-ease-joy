

# Content Pivot: "Fear of Looking Ridiculous While Sleeping"

## Overview

Full copywriting overhaul of the site to center the marketing angle on the **embarrassment of sleeping in public** -- drooling, mouth open, head flopping, weird positions, people taking photos of you. The design and structure stay the same; only the text content changes across 8 components.

---

## Changes by Component

### 1. Hero (`Hero.tsx`)
- Subtitle: "The anti-embarrassment travel pillow"
- Headline: **Stop waking up to *THAT photo.*"**
- Description: "Mouth open. Head on a stranger's shoulder. Drool on your shirt. We've all been there. Sleep&zy keeps your head locked in place so you sleep with dignity."
- CTA: **"Save your reputation — 50% OFF"**
- Social proof line stays (4.9/5 stars)
- Guarantee line: "90-day dignity-back guarantee"

### 2. Benefits (`Benefits.tsx`)
- Section title: "Why you'll never be *that person* again"
- 4 cards rewritten:
  - **"No More Head Drops"** -- Prevents the classic head-flop-onto-stranger moment
  - **"Mouth Stays Closed"** -- Chin support keeps your jaw in place. No drool, no open mouth
  - **"No Embarrassing Photos"** -- You look composed even when fully asleep
  - **"Discreet & Stylish"** -- Looks like a scarf, not a medical device. Nobody knows it's a pillow

### 3. ComparisonSlider (`ComparisonSlider.tsx`)
- Subtitle: "The proof speaks for itself"
- Title: **Embarrassing *vs.* Elegant**
- Labels: "EMBARRASSING" / "DIGNIFIED"

### 4. Testimonials (`Testimonials.tsx`)
- Section subtitle: "12,000+ dignified sleepers"
- Title: "Real stories, real *saves*"
- Rewrite all 5 testimonials around embarrassment stories:
  - Coworker took a photo of me drooling, went viral in the office...
  - Woke up on a stranger's shoulder on the subway...
  - My kids used to film me sleeping with my mouth open on road trips...
  - Client saw me passed out at the airport gate, mouth wide open...
  - Flight attendant woke me up because I was snoring with my head back...

### 5. SocialProofToasts (`SocialProofToasts.tsx`)
- Change message format to humorous/relatable:
  - "😴 Sarah L. from NYC just saved her dignity"
  - "🫣 James D. from LA — no more drool photos"
  - "✈️ Emily M. from Chicago — never embarrassed again"

### 6. BundleOffer (`BundleOffer.tsx`)
- Title: "Protect your *reputation*"
- Subtitle: "7 out of 10 customers save a friend from embarrassment too"
- CTA: "Save my dignity now"

### 7. FAQ (`FAQ.tsx`)
- Rewrite questions with the embarrassment angle:
  - "Will it really stop me from drooling?" / "Does it prevent my mouth from opening?" / "Will people notice I'm wearing it?" / "What if I'm a heavy sleeper who moves a lot?" / Keep washing + return + shipping questions

### 8. InactivityPopup (`InactivityPopup.tsx`)
- Headline: "WAIT! Your dignity is at stake"
- Body: "Next time you fall asleep in public, will you be ready?"
- CTA: "PROTECT MY REPUTATION NOW"
- Dismiss: "No thanks, I enjoy being photographed drooling"

### 9. UpsellPopup (`UpsellPopup.tsx`)
- Banner: "Complete your anti-embarrassment kit"
- Alert box: "The pillow prevents head drops. The headband blocks out light so you sleep deeper — without the zombie face."
